# Refund Policy

## Eligibility
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of the original purchase date. Annual plans are refundable within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are refundable only for the most recent billing cycle.

## What is not refundable
Usage-based overage charges and add-ons marked non-refundable are not eligible for a refund.

## How refunds are processed
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. Support agents cannot approve refunds above 500 USD. These requests must be escalated to the Billing team.
