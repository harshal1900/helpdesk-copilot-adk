# Data Export SOP

## Self-service export
Workspace admins can export data from Settings > Data > Export. Exports are available as CSV or JSON and are ready within 24 hours for workspaces under 10 GB.

## Large workspaces
Exports for workspaces of 10 GB or more take up to 72 hours and require a ticket to the Data team.

## Who can request an export
Only workspace admins can request an export. Requests from non-admin users must be redirected to their workspace admin.
