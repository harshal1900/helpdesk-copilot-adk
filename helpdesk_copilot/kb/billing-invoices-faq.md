# Billing and Invoices FAQ

## Downloading invoices
Workspace admins can download invoices from Billing > Invoices. Invoices are available for the last 24 months.

## Changing billing details
Workspace admins can update the billing email and company name under Billing > Details. Tax IDs cannot be changed after an invoice has been issued. A credit note is required in that case.

## Failed payments
After a failed payment the system retries 3 times over 7 days before the workspace is downgraded.
